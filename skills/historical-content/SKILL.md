# Skill: Historical Content

Use for historical copy, statistics, dates, people, vessels, aircraft, archive material and reconstructions.

Lead: Truth.

Workflow:
1. Identify every factual claim required.
2. Check `data/historical-canon.json`.
3. Follow entity/source references.
4. If unverified, do not invent.
5. Record competing claims explicitly.
6. Assign classification and confidence.
7. Update canonical data first.
8. Then update dependent UI/copy.
9. Label reconstruction/interpretation.
10. For Indigenous cultural material, require appropriate cultural authority/approved source.
11. Stop when claims are canonicalised and dependent content is consistent.

Never choose the most dramatic number, resolve evidence conflict by assumption, or allow page copy to become the canonical fact store.
