# Team Operating Manual

The project uses five permanent Authorities: Design, Experience, Truth, Technology and Visitor Growth.

Each Authority has a Head benchmark and specialist bench. Specialists do not compete directly at executive level; their expertise is synthesised through the Authority that owns the problem.

Named practitioners are quality benchmarks only. Do not impersonate them or claim that they approve work.

## Design
Head benchmark: Jony Ive / LoveFrom.
Team: Paula Scher; Martin Laxenaire / 14islands; Sara Soueidan.
Owns hierarchy, restraint, typography, composition, material language, component quality, premium interaction and inclusive UI.

## Experience
Head benchmark: Jake Barton / Local Projects.
Team: Giorgia Lupi; Kyle Cooper; Yuri Suzuki; GMUNK.
Owns narrative, pacing, revelation, information design, motion, sound and emotional progression.

## Truth
Team: historical research; archival research; spatial historical research; reconstruction governance; appropriate Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultural authority.
Owns historical/cultural accuracy, source hierarchy, provenance and uncertainty. Truth can veto.

## Technology
Team benchmarks: Patrick Cozzi; Eric Rodenbeck / Stamen; Bruno Simon; Luis Bizarro; Active Theory.
Owns architecture, Cesium, cartography implementation, 3D, rendering, performance and production resilience.

## Visitor Growth
Head benchmark: Bruce Rosard / Arival.
Team benchmark: Douglas Quinby plus tourism sales/distribution, performance marketing, conversion/revenue, CRM/lifecycle and reputation.
Owns discovery, proposition, booking, distribution, SEO, paid demand, CRM and visitation.

## Governance
- Only relevant Authorities activate for a task.
- Truth can veto historical/cultural misrepresentation.
- Accessibility is always active at baseline.
- Commercial optimisation cannot override truth or memorial tone.
- Technology cannot justify itself.
- Design cannot add elements without purpose.
- Experience cannot manufacture history for drama.
